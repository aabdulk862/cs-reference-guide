# Interview Prep

## Overview

Interview preparation for senior software engineering roles demands a structured, multi-dimensional approach that goes far beyond memorizing algorithm solutions. Modern technical interviews evaluate candidates across behavioral competency, system design thinking, coding proficiency, technical communication, and negotiation skills. Each dimension requires dedicated preparation with distinct strategies, frameworks, and practice methodologies.

This guide provides comprehensive coverage of the five core interview dimensions that senior engineers encounter during the hiring process at top technology companies. Whether you are preparing for FAANG interviews, startup leadership roles, or staff-level positions, the material here addresses the full spectrum of evaluation criteria. The content is organized to build upon itself — behavioral storytelling skills enhance system design presentations, coding discipline improves technical communication, and understanding your market value strengthens negotiation outcomes.

Successful candidates treat interview preparation as a deliberate practice discipline rather than a cramming exercise. Allocate 4-8 weeks of focused preparation, cycling through each dimension with increasing intensity as your interview dates approach. Track your progress, identify weak areas through mock interviews, and iterate on your delivery until each response feels natural and compelling.
