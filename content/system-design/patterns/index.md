5. [Data Pipeline Design](./data-pipeline-design.md) — Lambda/Kappa architecture, CDC, stream processing, and batch pipelines
6. [Design Patterns](./design-patterns.md) — GoF patterns and their application in distributed systems
7. [Common Interview Problems](./common-interview-problems.md) — URL shortener, chat system, notification service, and news feed design
